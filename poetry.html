Two maps of places connected to my interests. Discover poetry, art, food and screen locations on the culture map, then explore where I've been and where I'd like to go on the travel map.

A Map of My Interests

Open the layer menu to choose Poets, Artworks, Restaurants or Movies & TV Locations. Select a point to discover portraits and verses, artworks, featured dishes, or the real places behind familiar stories.

Nearby points merge into numbered clusters as you zoom out. Select a cluster to zoom in, or use the search button to find a name, film or series. Locations are approximate; film and television entries distinguish filming sites from places that inspired fictional settings.

My Travels & Wishlist

Some places I've already visited; others I'm still dreaming of. Open the layer menu to switch between Visited and Wishlist, then select a destination to read a short note.

Markers represent destinations rather than exact places visited. Most countries are represented by their capitals; Scotland is represented by Edinburgh.
